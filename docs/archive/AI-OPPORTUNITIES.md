# AI Opportunities

These are not Wave 1 features. They are observations about where AI adds genuine value once the underlying data exists. Each one is grounded in a specific pain, tied to specific Wave 1 records, and scoped to a job the parent currently does manually.

The pattern is the same in every case: Wave 1 builds the data. AI reads it. The parent reviews the output. Nothing is automated without review.

---

**PAIN-016 — Curriculum must become actionable work** *(Score 13)*
Addressed by: Add lesson/task (#13), Weekly planner structure (#11), Basic repeating weekly pattern (#17)

Every Sunday a parent sits with a curriculum guide and manually translates it into a week of lessons — subject by subject, child by child, blank field by blank field. AI that reads a curriculum document and drafts a week of lessons for parent review doesn't replace the parent's judgment; it replaces the blank page, which is where the hour goes.

---

**PAIN-011 — Parents want child-fit planning** *(Score 11)*
Addressed by: Progress by subject (#24), Completed lesson history (#25), Daily/weekly lesson list (#14)

The records Wave 1 builds — completion rates, skipped lessons, subject balance — are patterns a parent would see if they had time to look, and most don't. AI that reads those records and surfaces one observation ("Adam has skipped Math four of the last six weeks") gives the parent the awareness that should have been obvious without asking them to become their own analyst.

---

**PAIN-022 — Formal records need credibility** *(Score 14)*
Addressed by: Basic records report view (#33), Export records summary (#34), Records review checklist (#35)

A year-end narrative for a homeschool reviewer is an hour of writing from data the parent already has. AI that drafts "Khadijah completed 138 of 150 planned lessons across seven subjects, producing 31 portfolio items including writing samples, math workbook pages, and Quran recitation recordings" turns export day from a writing project into an editing task.

---

**PAIN-021 — Quran sessions need attendance proof** *(Score 11)*
Addressed by: Attendance record data model (#20), Mark attendance by child/date (#21), and the Wave 2B Quran native feature

Every hifz student is somewhere in a long memorization arc — new surahs, active revision, consolidation — and the teacher's judgment about what to review next currently lives in their head. Spaced-repetition logic applied to the session records Wave 1 captures surfaces "Al-Baqarah ayahs 1–20 is due for revision, last reviewed 18 days ago" — not as an AI recommendation but as a fact derived from the family's own data.

---

**PAIN-003 — Scattered records weaken confidence** *(Score 15)*
Addressed by: Parent dashboard shell (#8), Progress and attendance cards (#26), Basic records report view (#33)

The anxiety underneath scattered records is not that the parent forgot things — it's that they can never be certain they didn't. AI that monitors the records Wave 1 generates and proactively flags "you have three weeks of attendance unlogged for Zayd" converts a background worry into a specific, resolvable task.
