Sheath Academy — Landing Drafts

Three directions on the same concept. Open each, scroll slowly. They explore how far to lean cinematic vs. grounded. Version B is the recommended synthesis.

Version A — Cinematic

Maximal wow

The full experience: drifting starfield, the āyah, the constellation assembling into an eight-pointed star, the dashboard emerging from geometry. Highest risk, highest drama.

Version B — Grounded (recommended)

The synthesis

One tasteful hero moment, then real product views, the moat made explicit, trust and privacy, and a soft second path. Sakinah as tone — but it actually sells.

Version C — Still

Quietest extreme

Near-static, near-black, the āyah set perfectly, vast negative space, almost no motion. Tests whether less is the real wow.
